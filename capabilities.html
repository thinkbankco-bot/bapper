01 The list

A short list. Nothing on it is subcontracted.

Most of what a small service business needs falls into five areas. We keep the list short on purpose: we scope, build, and install it ourselves, and the same studio that built it answers the phone. If a request falls outside these five, we say so on the first call and point you to someone who does it.
