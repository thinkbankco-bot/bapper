ABOUT

A small studio,
built on our own work.

Bapper started as a set of tools built for our own projects — research pipelines, content systems, agents that run overnight and leave a report by morning. None of it was built to sell. It was built because we needed it, and it turned out other businesses had the same bottlenecks.

What we do

We build custom AI systems and automations for West Michigan businesses — workflow automation, assistants that know your business, integrations between the tools you already pay for, and marketing that's run as a measured system. Each engagement is scoped to how your business actually works.

How we think about the deal

Proven before you pay isn't a promotion; it's how we keep ourselves honest. Criteria get written before a build starts, numbers get shared as they come in, and if something isn't working we'd rather stop early than keep billing.

Who's here

Bapper is run by Jason out of Sparta, Michigan. It's a small studio on purpose — small enough that the person you talk to is the person doing the work, and honest enough to tell you when a problem doesn't need us.
