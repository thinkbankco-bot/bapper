01

02

What "working" means gets agreed up front, in plain language, so nobody has to argue about it later.

QUESTIONS

The fine print, out loud.

What's in the free assessment?

A written map of your business: where the hours go, where leads leak, how you show up in Google and in AI answers, and the one system worth building first. Keep it, act on it yourself, or ask us to. It isn't a disguised sales call.

What does a pilot cost?

A fixed quote from the assessment map — no hourly meters. Weeks, not quarters. Acceptance criteria are written before we start, and you pay when the system meets them.

What if it doesn't work?

The criteria are agreed up front. Miss them and the engagement ends there — you keep the map and anything shippable, with no invoice.

Do you do marketing too?

Yes — ads, Google rankings, and AI answers. We run them as a measured system with pass/fail rules, the same way we run everything else. See Marketing systems.

What about our data?

Scoped access, your accounts, and local-first options where the work allows it. Nothing leaves your systems that doesn't have to, and you can see exactly what does.

Is your work AI-generated? Do you disclose that?

We use AI where it's the right tool and follow every platform's disclosure rules. What we never do is ship something that only works because you didn't look closely.

Where are you actually located?

Sparta, Michigan — north of Grand Rapids. We serve West Michigan. If you want to meet the person doing the work over coffee before anything starts, that's normal here.
