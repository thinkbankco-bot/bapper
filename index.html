QUESTIONS

The fine print, out loud.

What does the free assessment cost? What's in it?

Nothing, and it isn't a disguised sales call. You get a written report covering the whole business, not just marketing: which competitor ads have run longest (that means they're paying off), where you show up in Google and in AI answers, what your reviews are doing for you, and where an automation could hand you your week back. Keep it, act on it yourself, or ask us to.

What does the free ad actually cost me?

Nothing. Not a call, not a card. We make it before we contact you. The catch is the obvious one: if it works, we'd like to be your ad shop.

What does “when it works” mean?

You run the ad on roughly $30–50/day for a week, in your own ad account. If it beats the pass/fail thresholds in the run plan that ships with your ad, we talk about a monthly engagement. Either way, the ad is yours to keep.

Who pays for the ad spend?

You do — your budget, your ad account, your data. We never touch client ad dollars. We're paid for the work, when the work works.

Is this AI-generated? Do you disclose that?

We use AI generation where it's the right tool and follow every platform's disclosure rules — TikTok's AIGC labels, Google's attestation, state law. What we never do is ship something that only works because you didn't look closely.

What's the engagement after a hit?

Monthly, month to month. No long-term contracts — the work re-earns the retainer or it doesn't.

Where are you actually located?

Sparta, Michigan — north of Grand Rapids. We serve West Michigan. If you want to meet the person doing the work over coffee before anything starts, that's normal here.
