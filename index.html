QUESTIONS

The fine print, out loud.

Why not just turn this on in the software I already pay for?

If ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, FieldEdge or Jobber already does this, it's switched on, and it's working — don't buy anything from me. I'll tell you that on the call and it costs you nothing; I'll spend ten minutes turning it on right and leave. Usually one of three things is true instead: it's in a tier you don't have, it fires one canned line that reads like a robot wrote it, or nobody ever turned it on. Ten minutes and I'll tell you which one you've got.

I already pay an answering service. Isn't this the same thing?

No, and it's not a replacement for one. An answering service takes a message from the people who wait on hold. This catches the ones who don't: the call gets a text back inside a minute, in your name, asking what broke and where — and the answer comes to your phone with the address already in it. It also chases your quotes, which no answering service does. If you've got a service and it's working, keep it. This sits in front of it.

I sell online. Shopify and Klaviyo already do some of this.

If your abandoned-cart flow is on, sending in your voice, and recovering — don't buy anything from me; I'll say that on the call. Where I get called is the stuff between the apps: the supplier invoice that never matches the PO, the Zap that broke in March and nobody noticed, the question that comes in at 9pm and needs your real prices and your real stock to answer. If a $30-a-month app does it, I'll tell you which one and leave.

What's the ninety-minute look?

Ninety minutes on the phone or at your table, and it's mostly you talking. We go through where the hours go, which calls and quotes are getting dropped, and how you show up when somebody searches or asks an AI who to call — and you get one page back with the one thing worth building first and a number next to it. It's free, and the page is yours either way. If the fix is a setting in software you already pay for, I'll turn it on and leave. It's a sales call in the sense that I'd like the work; it's not a sales call in the sense that you get the page either way.
