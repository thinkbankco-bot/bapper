07 Questions

Asked on most first calls.

Straight answers. If yours is not here, call or text (616) 227-4477.

Can't the software I already have do this?

Sometimes, yes. If a setting in something you already pay for solves it, we say so on the first call and show you where it is. We build only when nothing you own does the job.

How is this different from an answering service?

An answering service takes a message. This texts the caller back inside a minute under your business name, asks what is wrong and where, and puts the answer on your phone while you are still on the job.

What happens in the free assessment?

Ninety minutes, by phone or on site. We look at where the hours go and which calls and quotes get dropped. You get a one-page summary, and it is yours whether or not we work together.

Who owns the accounts and the phone number?

You do. Everything is built inside accounts in your name, phone number included. Business texting comes with carrier registration and consent rules. Setting those up is part of the install.

What if it doesn't perform?

The target is written down before work starts, and we measure against it on your own data. Nothing is final until it passes. After that it runs month to month, with a shutoff you control.

Is there a long contract?

No. Installations are fixed-scope. Service plans run month to month.

We run an online store. Is this for us?

Yes. A store has the same bottlenecks in different places: abandoned carts, order emails, the same five support questions. We work inside the store platform and the email tools you already use.

Do you handle marketing too?

Yes, run as measured systems: Google profile, local search, landing pages, ads. Pass and fail criteria are agreed before any spend.

Where are you, and who do you work with?

Sparta, Michigan. We work with owner-run businesses across West Michigan. On site when it helps, by phone when it doesn't.
