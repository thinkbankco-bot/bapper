How Bapper Labs texts you

Bapper Labs (Sparta, MI) sends texts only to people who ask for them. This page shows exactly how you opt in and what you'll receive.

How you opt in

There are three ways, and each one is your choice. Nothing else signs you up for texts: submitting the form without the box, calling us, or leaving a voicemail does not.

1. Check the box on our booking form

The box is optional and unchecked by default, and you can send the form without checking it. This is the box, directly above the Send it button:

2. Press 1 when you call and we can't answer

If you call (616) 227-4477 and we can't pick up, the greeting says:

"Hey, it's Jason at Bapper Labs. Press 1 and I'll text you right back about your call. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Or leave a message after the tone."

Pressing 1 is your request for a text. You get one text right away, and nothing more unless you reply. If you don't press 1, you can leave a voicemail and we'll call you back.

3. Text us first

If you text (616) 227-4477 first, we reply to your message in the same conversation.

Sample messages

  • "Hi, it's Jason at Bapper Labs. You asked for a text back. What can I help with? Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."
  • "Hi, it's Jason at Bapper Labs. Got your request about missed calls. Is now a good time to talk? Reply STOP to opt out."
  • "Bapper Labs: following up on your request. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

What you'll receive, and how to stop

  • Texts about your inquiry: replies, appointment confirmations, and follow-ups about work you asked about. Nothing promotional.
  • Replies may be sent by an automated assistant on behalf of Bapper Labs, and Jason follows up personally.
  • Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You'll get one confirmation and nothing further. Reply HELP for help, or call (616) 227-4477.
  • Consent to receive texts is not a condition of service.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
