How Bapper Labs texts you

Bapper Labs (Sparta, MI) replies by text to people who contact us first. This page shows exactly how that happens and what you agree to.

1. You call us

Our business line is (616) 227-4477. If we can't pick up, the voicemail says:

"You've reached Jason at Bapper Labs. I'm on a job. Leave your name and number and I'll text you back within a few minutes. Reply STOP to any text to opt out."

If you leave a message, you'll get one text from (616) 227-4477 like: "Hey, it's Jason with Bapper Labs. Sorry I missed you. What's going on? Text me here and I'll get right on it. Reply STOP to opt out."

2. You text us

Text (616) 227-4477 and we reply in the same conversation. The first reply to a new number includes: "This is Jason at Bapper Labs. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."

3. You use the contact form

The contact form on this site asks for your name, business, and mobile number, and shows this consent directly above the submit button:

Send it →

After submitting, you see: "You'll have a reply from me shortly. Check the number you gave me."

What you'll receive, and how to stop

  • Replies to your call or text, appointment confirmations, and follow-ups about work you asked about. Nothing promotional. We never text anyone who hasn't contacted us first.
  • Message frequency varies with the conversation. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You'll get one confirmation and nothing further. Reply HELP for help, or call (616) 227-4477.
  • We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
