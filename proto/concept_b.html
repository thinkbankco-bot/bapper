SHEET 05 / 06 General notes — how we work Notes 01–06 Rev 2026.09
General notes

A few things we hold ourselves to.

N01

Start with your workflow, not our tools.

We learn how the business actually runs before suggesting anything. The right system is usually smaller than people expect.

N02

Write the criteria before we start.

What "working" means gets agreed up front, in plain language, so nobody argues about it later.

N03

Show our numbers, good or bad.

Runs, costs, outcomes — logged and shared. If something isn't performing, you hear it from us first.

N04

Say no when it isn't worth building.

Some bottlenecks are better solved with a checklist than a system. We'll tell you when that's the case, free.

N05

Keep it in your accounts.

Your data, your logins, your ad spend. We work inside what you already own, with scoped access you can see.

N06

Stay close to the work.

Small studio. The person you talk to is the person who builds — and answers within a day.
