05 Questions

The questions everybody asks.

Why not just turn this on in the software I already pay for?

If ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, FieldEdge or Jobber already does this, it's switched on, and it's working — don't buy anything from us. We'll tell you that on the call and it costs you nothing; we'll spend ten minutes turning it on right and leave. Usually one of three things is true instead: it's in a tier you don't have, it fires one canned line that reads like a robot wrote it, or nobody ever turned it on. Ten minutes and we'll tell you which one you've got.

I already pay an answering service. Isn't this the same thing?

No, and it's not a replacement for one. An answering service takes a message from the people who wait on hold. This catches the ones who don't: the caller presses 1 and gets a text right back, in your name, asking what broke and where — and the answer comes to your phone with the address already in it. It also chases your quotes, which no answering service does. If you've got a service and it's working, keep it. This sits in front of it.

Whose number is it, and who's on the hook?

Your number and your business, registered in your name and portable to your own account whenever you want it. Every message carries your shop's name and STOP to opt out, because it is your shop. You approve every line of wording before anything sends, you can read everything that was sent any time you want, and you can shut the whole thing off from your phone in ten seconds — or one text to us and we do it.
