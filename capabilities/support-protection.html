04 / 04 Who it’s for

If this is your week.

Three situations we are called about most. If none of them are yours, the assessment is still free.

01

Your site went down on a Saturday and you found out from a customer.

Nobody was watching it. By the time anyone looked, it had been down since Thursday.

02

03

Questions

What we are asked first.

Will you support something you did not build?

Often yes. We start with an inventory and an honest assessment: some inherited systems are supportable as they are, and some need a fixed-scope repair before a plan makes sense. We tell you which before you commit to anything.

What counts as a change on request, and what becomes a new project?

Text and photo edits, wording, new pages on the existing structure, and small adjustments are in the plan. New systems, new integrations, and anything needing design work are quoted as their own fixed scope. The line is written into the plan so it is not an argument later.

What is your response time?

A reply within one business day, every time, and faster in practice for anything customer-facing. An outage gets worked immediately, not queued for the next scheduled window.

Can I cancel?

Month to month, no long-term contract. On the way out you keep the accounts, the domain, the source, and the runbook, because they were in your name the whole time.
