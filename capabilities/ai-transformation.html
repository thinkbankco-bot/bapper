04 / 04 Who it’s for

If this is your week.

Three situations we are called about most. If none of them are yours, the assessment is still free.

01

The phone rang four times before lunch and you called two of them back.

The other two booked somebody else and you will never know who they were. You need the caller answered inside a minute, not inside an afternoon.

02

There are eleven open quotes on the truck seat and none of them have been chased.

You meant to call. The week got in front of it. A follow-up that runs whether or not you remember is worth more than a better quote template.

03

The customer file has two thousand names in it and none of them have heard from you since the job.

You are buying new leads while the ones who already paid you sit in a spreadsheet.

Questions

What we are asked first.

Why not use the missed-call feature in the software I already pay for?

If ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, FieldEdge, or Jobber already does this, and it is switched on and working, you do not need us. We confirm that on the first call at no charge. Usually the feature sits in a tier you do not have, sends one generic line, or was never enabled.

Will it quote a price or promise a time on its own?

No. The system captures the problem, the address, and the name, and tells the caller a person will follow up. Pricing and scheduling stay with you. That limit is written into the proposal, not left to the wording.

What happens if it sends something wrong?

Every message is from a template you approved before launch, so there is nothing for it to invent. You can review every message sent, and you can shut the whole system off from your phone in seconds.

Does my customer data leave my system?

Records stay where they already are. Where a system needs transcription or document reading, we run it on local hardware wherever the work allows, which is how our own call system is built.
