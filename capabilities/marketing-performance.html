Questions

What we are asked first.

Will you tell me what results to expect?

Not as a number. We have no client results to quote and we will not invent a forecast. What we will do is write the target the campaign has to hit and the date it is judged on, before you spend anything, so the argument afterwards is about evidence rather than opinion.

What happens if it fails the criteria?

We say so, in the weekly summary, in writing. Then we either change one variable and re-judge, or stop the channel. We do not keep a failing channel alive because it is billable.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Marketing runs month to month under the same engagement model as everything else. Accounts, budgets, and ad platforms are in your name, so nothing is stranded if you stop.

Is AI search visibility worth paying attention to yet?

Enough of your customers now ask an assistant who to call that we check it monthly and record what comes back. It is a small share of the work, and it is included rather than sold as its own product.
