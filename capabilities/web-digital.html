Questions

What we are asked first.

Do I own the site and the domain?

Yes. The domain stays registered to your business, hosting is in your name, and the source is yours. If you leave, you take all of it. We do not hold a site hostage as a retention tool.

Can I edit it myself?

Text and photo changes are part of the monthly service plan: send the change by text and it is live within two business days. If you would rather edit it yourself, we will say so honestly when your page count makes that worth the extra setup.

Is a rebuild always the answer?

No. Plenty of sites need a structure fix, correct listings, and three new pages rather than a rebuild. The audit says which, and it says so before there is a proposal to sign.

What is GEO, and is it different from SEO?

Search engine work aims at a ranked list of links. Answer engine work aims at being one of the two or three names an assistant says out loud when someone asks who to call. The underlying work overlaps; the targets are different, and we track both.
