HOW ENGAGEMENTS WORK

Three shapes. No mush.

1

The ninety-minute look

We map the workflow, name the one automation worth building first, and hand you a fixed quote. On me for the businesses that let me put their name on the result. If you build it with someone else, keep the map.

2

The Pilot

One system, fixed scope, weeks not quarters — with acceptance criteria defined up front. “You pay when it works,” applied to software.

3

Run & extend

Monthly: the system maintained, extended, and watched by the person who built it. Month to month, like everything here.

If you need a large transformation program, we're probably not the right fit — and we'll say so. If you need one working system this month, that's what we do.
