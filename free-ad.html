QUESTIONS
Why free? What's the angle?

The angle is the obvious one, stated out loud: most agencies sell promises, we'd rather sell a result that already happened. If our free ad makes you money, hiring us stops being a leap of faith. If it doesn't, we've cost you nothing and learned something.

What do you need from me to build it?

Usually nothing — what's public on your site and listings is enough for the first cut. If you have footage of your team or your work, one phone clip makes it even better, but it's optional.

Who runs the ad?

You do, in your own ad account, with your budget. We supply setup instructions and the kill rules. We never touch your ad dollars.

Is it really AI-made?

Built by our pipeline, finished by a human, disclosed where platforms require it. Judge it the only way that matters: watch it and ask if you'd stop scrolling.

What does the retainer cost, if it hits?

Monthly, month to month, scoped to what your business needs — discussed after the ad earns the conversation. No long-term contracts, ever.
